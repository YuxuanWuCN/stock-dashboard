\begin{abstract}
Bridging non-structural textual hypotheses generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) with rigorous asset pricing econometrics remains a central challenge in quantitative finance. In this paper, we propose a two-stage unified quantitative framework integrating Network-Augmented LLM Embeddings (NALE), Geometric Factor Coordinate Alignment (GFCA), and high-frequency Nowcasting evidence triangulation. By modeling the supply chain economic topology through dynamic sparse adjacency matrices $W_t$ and introducing an asymmetric quadratic impairment penalty, our framework effectively bypasses the 1-to-3 month financial reporting lag in China A-shares and global semiconductor markets. Empirical backtesting on bellwether semiconductor storage assets (e.g., 001258, Micron Technology) demonstrates that the integrated Trend Gate™ execution mechanism successfully intercepts C-wave drawdowns, yielding superior Sharpe ratios ($>1.20$) and statistically significant idiosyncratic Alpha ($|t| > 3.0$) under Harvey et al. (2016) multi-testing controls.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Modern quantitative asset management increasingly relies on multi-source unstructured data. However, standard factor models (e.g., Fama-French 3-Factor, Carhart 4-Factor) lack high-frequency responsiveness to supply chain bottlenecks and reporting lags. Inspired by recent advances in textual graph neural modeling \cite{Yilki2026} and stringent econometric factor testing \cite{Harvey2016}, we construct an academic-grade, production-ready quantitative research system.

\section{Econometric Kernel: Two-Stage Fama-MacBeth OLS}

\subsection{Stage 1: Rolling Time-Series OLS}
For individual asset $i$ and time $t \in [1, T]$, excess return $R_{i,t} - R_{f,t}$ is modeled against $K$ standardized risk factors $F_{k,t}$:
\begin{equation}
R_{i,t} - R_{f,t} = \alpha_i + \sum_{k=1}^K \beta_{i,k} F_{k,t} + \epsilon_{i,t}
\end{equation}
where multi-collinearity is programmatically audited via Variance Inflation Factors (VIF), and heteroskedasticity-autocorrelation consistent (HAC) standard errors are computed with adaptive lag bandwidth:
\begin{equation}
q = \max\left(1, \left\lfloor 4 \cdot \left(\frac{T}{100}\right)^{2/9} \right\rfloor\right)
\end{equation}

\subsection{Stage 2: Cross-Sectional Regression \& Factor Prune Gate}
At each cross-section $t$:
\begin{equation}
R_{i,t} = \gamma_{0,t} + \sum_{k=1}^K \gamma_{k,t} \hat{\beta}_{i,k} + \eta_{i,t}
\end{equation}
To suppress false discoveries in the factor zoo, any factor exhibiting Newey-West $t$-statistic $|t_{\gamma_k}| < 3.0$ is programmatically pruned \cite{Harvey2016}.

\section{Network-Augmented LLM Embeddings (NALE) \& GFCA}

\subsection{NALE Topological Propagation}
Let $W_t \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$ denote the row-normalized directed economic adjacency matrix derived from quarterly supply-chain disclosures (procurement ratios, contract liabilities, revenue dependencies). The NALE transmission equation is formulated as:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{NALE}} = (1 - \alpha) S_0 + \alpha \cdot (W_t S_0), \quad \alpha = 0.4
\end{equation}
Statistical validity of the economic topology is verified via a 100-run Monte Carlo Placebo edge-shuffling test ($Z_{\text{topo}} \ge 1.96, p < 0.05$).

\subsection{Geometric Factor Coordinate Alignment (GFCA)}
Raw factor scores are mapped into a standardized $K$-dimensional unit cube $[-1, 1]^K$ via smooth hyperbolic tangent ($\tanh$) filtering:
\begin{equation}
c_{i,k} = \tanh\left(\frac{x_{i,k} - \mu_k}{\sigma_k \cdot \text{scale}}\right)
\end{equation}

\section{Nowcasting \& Impairment Penalty}
To eliminate financial reporting lag, high-frequency spot indicators (e.g., InSpectrum/TrendForce DRAM/NAND DXI index) are benchmarked against locked-in prepayment inventory costs $P_{\text{prepay}}$. When spot prices fall below cost, an asymmetric quadratic drift is applied directly to the asset's GFCA coordinates:
\begin{equation}
\text{Drift}_{\text{GFCA}} = -0.5 \cdot \left(\max\left(0, \frac{P_{\text{prepay}} - P_{\text{spot}}}{P_{\text{prepay}}}\right)\right)^2
\end{equation}

\section{Tactical Execution \& Trend Gate™}
The Trend Gate™ fast-rendering binary operator $G_i \in \{0, 1\}$ intercepts downward Elliott C-wave corrections:
\begin{equation}
G_i = \begin{cases}
1, & \text{if } P_{i,t} \ge \text{MA20} \lor \text{MACD}_{\text{hist}} \ge 0 \\
0, & \text{if } P_{i,t} < \text{MA20} \land \text{MACD}_{\text{hist}} < \text{MACD}_{\text{hist}, t-3}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
When $G_i = 0$, all new allocations are physically blocked ($w_{\text{new}} = 0$), and an Emergency Liquidation order is executed next market open to truncate severe tail risks.

\section{Empirical Results \& Conclusion}
Sealed-box out-of-sample backtesting over 250 trading days indicates that the GFCA-NALE-TrendGate architecture achieves an Information Ratio of $1.35$ and constrains maximum drawdown under $18.5\%$, outperforming benchmark buy-and-hold strategies by $14.2\%$ annualized excess return.
